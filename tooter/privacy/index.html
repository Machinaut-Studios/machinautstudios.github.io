Privacy Policy for Tooter

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Tooter ("the app") is developed and published by Machinaut Studios LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: Tooter keeps everything on your device. The only exception is the optional G.U.S.T.E. Network, which is off until you turn it on and is described below.

Information we collect

If you never turn on the G.U.S.T.E. Network, none. Tooter has no analytics and no advertising identifiers, and nothing you record leaves your device.

If you join the G.U.S.T.E. Network, Tooter sends the data described in the next section to a server we run. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

The G.U.S.T.E. Network (optional)

The Global Unified Seismic Tooter Emissions Network is an optional public globe and set of leaderboards at tooter.earth. It is controlled by three switches in Settings, all off by default, and you can turn any of them off at any time.

  • Note where I toot stays on your device. When it is on, Tooter asks for your location once each time you keep a recording and stores the city, country, a coarse area of about 250 km² and your altitude with that recording. Your exact coordinates are never stored or sent. Nothing in this option leaves your device.
  • Report to the Globe sends each toot you keep to the Network with its city, country and coarse area. It appears on the public globe anonymously: the city is shown, your Handle and exact location are not.
  • Join the Leaderboards ranks your toots under a Handle the Network makes up for you (for example "Thunderous Walrus-417"). Leaderboards show Handles and scores, never places.

For each toot you keep while one of the sharing switches is on, Tooter sends:

  • its shape: a list of loudness values over time, which the server uses to check the Couric Score;
  • how long it lasted and when it was recorded;
  • its city, country and coarse area, only if Report to the Globe is on.

The audio itself is never sent. When you first join, your device proves to Apple (App Attest) or Google (Play Integrity) that it is running the genuine Tooter app, and the Network gives your install a random ID and your Handle.

How long we keep it. Toot shapes are kept for 30 days, except for toots currently in a leaderboard's top 100. Individual toots are kept for 90 days, after which they are folded into totals (such as best score, streak days and city counts) and their place is deleted. Backups are kept for 14 days.

Leaving. Settings → Leave the GUSTE Network deletes your Handle, your toots and your personal totals from our server straight away; backups age out within 14 days. City and country counts that do not identify anyone remain. Settings also shows a recovery code that moves your Handle to a new device; anyone holding the code can do the same, so keep it private.

The data is used only to run the tooter.earth globe and its rankings. It is never sold.

Microphone and audio recordings

Tooter requests microphone access (RECORD_AUDIO) so you can record audio clips. All recordings:

  • are created and saved only on your device;
  • are never uploaded, transmitted, or sent off your device by the app;
  • remain entirely under your control and can be deleted at any time from within the app.

We never receive, access, or have any copy of your recordings.

Purchases

Tooter offers a single optional in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by the store you downloaded the app from — Apple's App Store on iOS, or Google Play Billing on Android. We do not receive or store your payment details, card information, or billing address. On iOS, payment handling is governed by the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions and the Apple Privacy Policy; on Android, by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The app stores only a local flag indicating whether the purchase was made.

Location

Tooter asks for location permission only when you turn on Note where I toot, and only while the app is in use. It reads one position per kept recording, turns it into a city, country and coarse area on your device, and discards the coordinates.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data. The G.U.S.T.E. Network runs on Google Cloud and is served through Cloudflare, which process it on our behalf. Device attestation is checked with Apple or Google as described above.

Children's privacy

Tooter does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional G.U.S.T.E. Network asks for no name or contact details. The app contains crude/toilet humor and is intended for a general audience.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@machinautstudios.com.
