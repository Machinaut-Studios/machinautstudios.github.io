Slateveil — Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026 · Machinaut Studios LLC

The short version

Slateveil keeps your game on your device. There is no account, no login and no server of ours to talk to — every wall you play is inside the download. The one thing that leaves your phone is what the advertising SDK collects in order to show ads, described below.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your coins, which walls you have finished and what you scored, your Daily record and your streak are stored in your device's own app storage. They are never transmitted anywhere, and uninstalling the app deletes them. We cannot read them, and we cannot restore them for you.

Advertising

Slateveil shows ads through Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier and technical information about the device, and shares it with advertising partners in order to select and measure ads. That identifier is not linked to your name or to any account, and Slateveil does not track you across other apps or websites — there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to ask for.

You can reset or limit that identifier at any time in your device settings: on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising, and on Android under Settings → Google → Ads.

Google's own description of what it collects is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

You will be asked, before any ad loads, whether you consent to personalised advertising. You can decline, and you can change your answer later from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app. Declining does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are not personalised.

Purchases

Slateveil sells Remove Ads and three coin packs. Those transactions are handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see your payment details, and we receive only whether a purchase succeeded so the app can unlock what you bought. Removing ads stops the interruptions; it does not change any of this policy, because there was nothing else being collected.

Children

Slateveil is not directed at children under 13. Ad content is capped at a general audience rating.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the version in the app stores is updated at the same time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@machinautstudios.com.
