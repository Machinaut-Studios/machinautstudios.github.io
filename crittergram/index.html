Critter Gram

Type it. A critter says it.
Fifteen animals who deliver your message badly, on purpose.

Write one line. Pick an animal. Critter Gram hands it back as a silly animal-voiced video — a sprite critter whose mouth flaps in time with the audio — ready for Messages, WhatsApp, or anywhere else you share things. Every voice is synthesized on your phone. No account, no analytics, and not one network call between typing the message and sharing the video.

▶ SPEC SHEET

WHAT IT DOES

A gram is something a critter brings you on somebody's behalf. There are always three people in one: you, the animal, and whoever is listening. The animal is the one in the middle, and it is not good at its job. Here is the whole machine.

  • FEATURE

    FIFTEEN PERSONALITIES

    Personality is innate — each critter writes its own way. The chipmunk relays you verbatim. The turtle tells you a story from his day first. Same message, fifteen wildly different deliveries.

  • FEATURE

    IT NEVER ANSWERS YOU

    The critter carries the message; it does not reply to it. Ask "fancy a coffee?" and the question arrives as a question. Left alone, a model would happily decline on your behalf. We stopped it.

  • FEATURE

    MADE ON YOUR PHONE

    Offline speech synthesis, a tape-speed pitch trick per animal, mouth sprites driven by the audio's own loudness, and a hardware-encoded video. Nothing leaves the device to make it.

  • FEATURE

    SHARES LIKE A VIDEO

    Out comes an ordinary video file through the ordinary share sheet. The recipient needs nothing installed, no account, and no explanation beyond the obvious one.

  • FEATURE

    OR A FEW HUNDRED BYTES

    When both people have the app, skip the video: the gram travels as a scrap of text and the other phone rebuilds the voice and the animation locally. Friends pair by invite link only.

  • OPTIONAL

    THE BIG BRAIN

    A language model you can add later, which runs entirely on your device and writes the generative critters' material. Skip it and everything still works — a built-in procedural engine covers for it.

▶ PERSONNEL FILE

THE ROSTER

One-shot the whole staff. Kept every take.

Fifteen animals are on staff. Four are free to send with; the other eleven arrive with a single one-time unlock — no subscription. Receiving is never gated: a gram plays in full on any phone, every critter, paid or not. Six representative units follow.

  • UNIT 11  //  SNAIL

    SAYS NOTHING

    Speaks not one word. Hands over a written letter instead — and sent to a friend who has the app, it takes three real days to arrive. This is the intended behavior and we will not be moving it.

  • UNIT 12  //  GOLDFISH

    STARTS OVER, REPEATEDLY

    Forgets halfway and goes back to the top. Forgets again. Gets a little further each time, and does land it in the end, which is the remarkable part.

  • UNIT 04  //  DUCK

    ONLY QUACKS

    Just quacks, honestly and at length. The message rides alongside as a decodable quack transcript — paste it into the in-app translator to get your words back.

  • UNIT 10  //  JACK RABBIT

    FOUR WORDS, TOTAL

    Two sentences. Two words each. Four words, total, enforced by two very small boxes. Brevity is not a style here, it is a hard constraint.

  • UNIT 09  //  WHALE

    ONE UNBROKEN SONG

    Takes the plain delivery and stretches it into a single deep song with no gaps in it anywhere. Accurate. Extremely long.

  • UNIT 15  //  DODO

    WORDS OF ONE SYLLABLE

    Says all of it, but only in words of one beat. Nuance does not survive the trip. Neither, usually, does the name of whoever sent it.

▶ ON THE RECORD

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS

There is no account to make and no profile to fill in. Grams are built on your phone and shared by you, to whoever you choose. Sending straight to a friend does pass through one small relay — which holds no names, never reads what it carries, and deletes each gram the moment your friend's phone confirms it arrived. The full, plainly-worded details live on the privacy page — the same one we submit to the App Store and Google Play.
