HUMANS IN THE LOOP

Here is the part we are not joking about. The code is vibe-coded and we will say so anywhere you like. The art and the music are not. Every sprite, every icon, every track in our games was made by a person who was commissioned, paid, and credited by name. We think the machines are very good at the tedious half and conspicuously bad at the half that makes you feel something — so we buy that half from humans, on purpose, at human prices.

    Drawn by an artist with a name, an invoice, and opinions about our feedback. No generated art ships in our games.

    Written and performed by composers we pay. The GPU has never once been asked for a melody, and it is better this way for everyone.

    Names ship inside every build, not buried in a settings page nobody opens. If a human made it, a human is on it.
