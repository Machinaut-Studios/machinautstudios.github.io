▶ SPEC SHEET

WHAT IT DOES

Most voice tools stop at a single shortcut. VoiceCmd is built for everything past that point.

Macros, not single shortcuts

Hold a key down for a set duration, then release it. Chain any number of steps with delays you control. Mix keys, combinations, clicks, movement, scrolling and typed text in one command.

The whole keyboard

F1 through F24, the numpad, the navigation cluster, media keys and chorded combinations — delivered as real input, not as text pasted into a box.

Listens when you want it to

Push-to-talk or a toggle hotkey, so it will not fire mid-sentence while you are on a call. An optional wake word if you would rather address it by name. The hotkey gates matching rather than the microphone, so there is no spin-up delay.

Profiles that follow you

Group commands per application. VoiceCmd notices which app you switch to and loads the matching profile automatically. Export any profile as JSON to back it up or share it.

Fast, and honest about it

Roughly half a second from the moment you stop speaking to the keystroke landing. A built-in debug view breaks the timing down step by step, so you can check that claim rather than take our word for it.

Understands you, not just your diction

Fuzzy matching tolerates typos and homophones. Longest phrase wins, so “reload weapon” never fires “reload” by mistake. Per-command and global cooldowns stop one utterance firing twice.

▶ YOUR DATA

IT NEVER LEAVES YOUR MACHINE

The speech model is bundled inside the app and runs on your own computer. Audio is processed as a live stream, never written to a file, and discarded the moment it becomes text. There are no accounts, no telemetry and no servers — VoiceCmd works with the network switched off. Read the full privacy policy.

▶ SUPPORT

SOMETHING NOT WORKING?

Email info@machinautstudios.com and we will help. A real person reads it.

Tell us this and it will be quicker

Open Settings → Debug Info and copy the block — it holds the version, the exact build, and your operating system. Select it, copy it, and paste it into the email.

Nothing is being typed (macOS)

macOS silently discards simulated keystrokes until the app is allowed to send them. Grant VoiceCmd Accessibility in System Settings → Privacy & Security. The app shows an INPUT BLOCKED chip while that permission is missing.

It is not hearing you

Open Settings → Microphone input and watch the level meter while you speak. If it never moves, the app is being handed silence — pick the correct input device there, and check the microphone is not muted in your system sound settings.
